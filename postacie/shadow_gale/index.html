Shadow Gale / Mamori Totoyama

Shadow Gale

Shadow Gale (シャドウゲール), also known by her real name Mamori Totoyama (魚山護), is one of the main characters of Magical Girl Raising Project: Restart, Magical Girl Raising Project: ACES, and Magical Girl Raising Project: QUEENS. She also had minor roles in Magical Girl Raising Project: Limited, Magical Girl Raising Project: JOKERS, and Magical Girl Raising Project: White. She is Pfle's partner and butler in real life, her family having served the latter's for many generations. However, over time, she becomes increasingly distrustful of her mistress.

As a Magical Girl, Shadow Gale has short, wavy, dirty blonde hair that becomes a lighter blonde at the ends. Her bangs are swept to the right, nearly covering her eye. Her eyes are yellow, with slit-styled irises. On both sides of her head, she wears large X-shaped clips. Her cap is shaped like a nurse’s hat, which has a back pocket and a small bird design on the front. She also has Dark crow bird feathers resting against the back of her head which are tucked into her collar. Shadow Gale wears a dark, nurse-like outfit with a black top and skirt. The top layer of her outfit sticks out, with large black bows on the ends that are able to hold her scissors and wrench. She also has two black belts wrapped around both of her biceps, and black gloves with little bows. The top of her dress reaches up to her neck, and has a dark bow tied to it in the back. Pinned to her dress via a black cross-shaped pin is a white name tag with black scribble. She also wears a belt around her left thigh with a small bag attached to it. Shadow Gale has a large crow bird’s tail, and wears black high heel shoes. Wrapped around her calves are black bows.

Opis postaci

Shadow Gale is loyal to Pfle, as part of the family that has served the Hitokouji for generations. Shadow Gale is willing to modify machines for Pfle without outward complaints, though she will make snarky comments from time to time, and follows her orders. She refers to Pfle as "my lady." It is shown that she is frequently annoyed by Pfle's behavior and actions, but very rarely voices this opinion due to her position as Pfle's servant. Gale also hates Pfle's pranks and was enraged when she secretly drew on her face at school. Despite this, Gale still truly cares for Pfle, as shown when began to breakdown after believing she had killed Pfle, believing her whole entire life should be revolved around serving her. Shadow Gale is generally a polite person to everyone and dislikes violence between people.
