Clantail / Nene Ono

Magical Daisy

Clantail (クランテイル), also known by her real name Nene Ono (尾野 寧々), is one of the main protagonists of Magical Girl Raising Project: Restart and Magical Girl Raising Project: breakdown. She is the leader of Team Clantail. While she is reserved and quiet, it's easy to distinguish her true feelings by looking at her animal half's movement.

As a Magical Girl, Clantail has short, dark brown hair with bangs parted in the middle and pointed hair buns on either side of her head. Attached to the ends of the buns are what appear to be red jewels with gold edges and a pink flower can be seen floating above her head. She has both a pair of small antlers and large antlers, with pink shapes floating above the larger pair. Tied to her antlers are purple tassels. Her eyes are two-toned, with the upper half being red and the lower half being a pale orange. She can be seen wearing red eyeshadow and red hoop earrings. She also has a red triangle pattern around her neck. She wears a top resembling that of a kimono or yukata, with a black edging, a red haneri, and frills connected to the edges of the haneri. The base color of the top is beige with wavy, purple horizontal stripes. Attached to the front of her outfit is a large, hot pink bow, and smaller bows of the same color are attached to the bottom of her sleeves. Dark purple flower decoration's are attached to her shoulders, and dark purple feathers peak out from underneath her sleeves. There are multiple pink and purple tail-like decorations also attached to the lower end of her top, and beige, frilly fabric hangs from underneath her top in the front. An illustration in Restart (Part 2) shows her with colored nails, although it is currently unknown what color they are. Clantail’s lower half changes depending on the animal she transforms into, but she is commonly depicted as having transformed as either a horse or deer.

Opis postaci

Clantail has a calm and stern demeanor and rarely shows any emotion, even when under pressure. In spite of this, she still displays consideration for her fellow teammates and does her best to break up arguments between Rionetta and Nonako. These qualities eventually earned her the natural position of team leader. While Clantail seems emotionless at first glance, you can actually distinguish her feelings by the half of her that is an animal. For example, when she's a deer, Pechka notices Clantail's tail wagging while eating one of her cooked meals. In reality, Clantail’s emotionless demeanor is simply due to the fact that she doesn't know how to interact with other people. This is because she cannot understand other's mindsets, as everyone thinks differently. On the other hand, she feels she can relate more to animals, though says she doesn't particularly love them and sees them as average creatures. After she thinks this, she muses to herself that maybe she does love animals, in comparison to how she feels about people. Clantail's dream is to become a zoologist. After the events of Restart, Clantail decides to become more sociable. She begins to learn how to cook and interact more with her classmates.
